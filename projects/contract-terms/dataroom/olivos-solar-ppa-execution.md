# Part I — Individual Terms

The elections in this Part I form part of the General Provisions in Part II. Where Part I and Part II conflict, Part I prevails.

**Parties.** Seller: Olivos Solar Proyecto Dos S.L., a special purpose company owning the Facility. Buyer: Meseta Energía Comercializadora S.A. The Buyer is a licensed electricity trader and no guarantee of the Buyer's obligations is provided by any affiliate of the Buyer. The Buyer's obligations are supported solely by the letter of credit described in section 9.2 of Part II.

**Facility.** Facility: a ground-mounted solar photovoltaic plant with an installed capacity of 90 MWp (direct current) and a grid export capacity of 75 MW (alternating current) located at Valdeolivos in the Province of Badajoz, Spain, as further described in Annex 1.

**Supply Period.** Supply Period: twelve (12) years commencing on the Commercial Operation Date.

**Target Commercial Operation Date.** 30 June 2027. Longstop Date: 31 December 2027.

**Product.** Electricity generated by the Facility and delivered at the Delivery Point, together with all Guarantees of Origin issued in respect of that electricity. No capacity product, ancillary service or other attribute is sold under this Agreement.

**Contract Quantity and volume structure.** Pay-as-produced. The Buyer purchases one hundred per cent (100%) of the Metered Output during the Supply Period. There is no minimum or maximum annual quantity. Expected Annual Production: 165,000 MWh (P50). The Expected Annual Production is an estimate, not a delivery obligation.

**Price.** Contract Price: EUR 38.75 per MWh, fixed for the Supply Period without indexation. The negative price rule in section 7.4 of Part II applies.

**Settlement.** Physical, at the Delivery Point (section 4.3 of Part II).

**Delivery Point and Balancing.** Delivery Point: the 220 kV connection point at the Valdeolivos substation. The Buyer is the market representative of the Facility and the balance responsible party for the Metered Output from the Commercial Operation Date (section 5.2 of Part II).

**Credit Support.** Seller: performance bond of EUR 1,200,000 from the Signing Date until the Commercial Operation Date (Annex 2). Buyer: letter of credit equal to three (3) months of expected payments under this Agreement (Annex 3), from the Commercial Operation Date until the end of the Supply Period. No parent company guarantee is provided by either Party.

**Governing law.** Spanish law. Disputes are resolved by arbitration seated in Madrid (section 20 of Part II).

**Annexes.** Annexes 1 to 3 are attached and form part of this Agreement. Annex 4 is not used.

# Part II — General Provisions

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Terms defined in Part I or in a section of Part II have that meaning throughout this Agreement. In addition:

Availability means, for a Contract Year, the percentage of Daylight Hours in which the Facility was technically able to export at the Delivery Point at not less than ninety-five per cent (95%) of its irradiance-adjusted capacity, calculated under Annex 1, excluding hours affected by Force Majeure or by unavailability of the grid connection not caused by the Seller.

Business Day means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday in Madrid.

Commercial Operation Date means the date on which the conditions in section 3.4 have been met and the Seller has issued the notice described there.

Contract Year means each period of twelve (12) consecutive months commencing on the Commercial Operation Date or an anniversary of it; the last Contract Year ends with the Supply Period.

Daylight Hours means the hours between official sunrise and sunset at the site of the Facility in which irradiance measured on site is at least 50 W/m2.

Guarantee of Origin means a certificate issued by the national regulatory authority or its designated issuing body certifying that electricity has been generated from a renewable source, and any successor certificate.

Insolvency Event means, in respect of a Party, that it is declared insolvent, is the subject of an insolvency petition not dismissed within sixty (60) days, enters into a composition with its creditors, has an administrator appointed over substantially all of its assets, or is wound up.

Interest Rate means the rate of the European Central Bank for its main refinancing operations plus four (4) percentage points per annum.

Lender means any financial institution providing debt finance to the Seller for the Facility, and any agent acting for it.

Longstop Date means 31 December 2027.

Metered Output means the electricity delivered at the Delivery Point in a settlement period, as measured under section 6.

Permits means all licences, authorisations, registrations and consents required by law for the Facility and the sale of its electricity.

Target Commercial Operation Date means 30 June 2027.

1.2 Headings do not affect interpretation. References to sections are to sections of Part II. References to a law include it as amended or replaced. Amounts are in euro; quantities in MWh; times are local time at the site.

## 2. Conditions Precedent

2.1 The obligations of the Parties under sections 3 to 9 are conditional on the following (the "Conditions Precedent"):

(a) the Seller has obtained the administrative authorisation for construction of the Facility;

(b) the Seller has entered into its connection agreement with the transmission system operator and holds access and connection permits for 75 MW at the Delivery Point;

(c) the Seller has delivered the performance bond under section 9.1;

(d) the Buyer has delivered a copy of its licence as an electricity trader and evidence of its registration with the market operator and the transmission system operator as market participant and balance responsible party.

2.2 The Seller is responsible for conditions (a) to (c) and the Buyer for condition (d). Each Party shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy its conditions and shall notify the other when each is satisfied. A Party may waive a condition for which it is not responsible.

2.3 If the Conditions Precedent have not been satisfied or waived by 30 September 2026, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice with immediate effect, without liability to the other; originals of any credit support shall be returned.

## 3. Construction, Milestones and Commercial Operation

3.1 The Seller shall design, procure, construct, test and commission the Facility at its own cost and risk, in accordance with good industry practice, the Permits, its connection agreement and Annex 1.

3.2 The Seller shall report in writing to the Buyer on construction progress at the end of each calendar quarter until the Commercial Operation Date, stating the status of the indicative milestones (financial close by 31 July 2026; start of construction by 30 September 2026; mechanical completion by 30 April 2027; first energisation by 31 May 2027) and its current expectation of the Commercial Operation Date. Slippage against indicative milestones is not a breach.

3.3 The Seller may deliver test electricity before the Commercial Operation Date. The Buyer shall take it, act as market representative for it under section 5.2 and pay for it at the Contract Price, subject to section 7.4.

3.4 The Commercial Operation Date occurs when the Seller has given the Buyer written notice, with supporting certificates, that:

(a) the Facility holds the definitive operating authorisation and is registered as a production facility;

(b) the transmission system operator has confirmed that the Facility may export up to 75 MW at the Delivery Point;

(c) the metering under section 6 has been installed and verified; and

(d) at least sixty (60) MW of alternating current export capacity has been commissioned and has exported for seventy-two (72) continuous hours, as certified by an independent engineer reasonably acceptable to the Buyer.

The Seller shall give at least thirty (30) days' notice of the expected Commercial Operation Date.

3.5 If the Commercial Operation Date has not occurred by the Target Commercial Operation Date of 30 June 2027, the Seller shall pay the Buyer liquidated damages of EUR 6,000 for each day of delay, up to an aggregate cap of EUR 1,500,000. These liquidated damages are the Buyer's sole remedy for delay until the Longstop Date and are invoiced monthly in arrears under section 7.6.

3.6 The Target Commercial Operation Date and the Longstop Date are extended day for day while construction or commissioning is prevented by Force Majeure or by a delay of the transmission system operator under the connection agreement, provided the Seller notifies the Buyer within five (5) Business Days of the event, up to an aggregate of one hundred and eighty (180) days.

3.7 If the Commercial Operation Date has not occurred by the Longstop Date, as extended, the Buyer may terminate this Agreement by written notice. The Seller shall then pay any unpaid delay damages; no Termination Amount is payable.

## 4. Delivery and Acceptance

4.1 The Seller shall deliver to the Buyer, and the Buyer shall take, all electricity generated by the Facility and delivered at the Delivery Point. The Buyer shall purchase one hundred per cent (100%) of the Metered Output of the Facility during the Supply Period. The Seller shall not sell electricity generated by the Facility to any third party during the Supply Period.

4.2 The Seller bears all costs and losses up to the Delivery Point, including grid access charges payable by generators. The Buyer bears all costs and losses from the Delivery Point onward, including the costs of its role under section 5.2.

4.3 Title to and risk in the electricity pass to the Buyer at the Delivery Point. The Seller warrants that the electricity is free of any lien, charge or third-party claim.

4.4 The Seller does not commit to any minimum quantity. Its obligation is to operate and maintain the Facility in accordance with good industry practice and to meet the Availability guarantee in section 5.6.

4.5 The Buyer shall accept delivery in whatever quantity is generated and shall not refuse delivery because it has no use for the electricity or because the market price is below the Contract Price. The Buyer may sell the electricity on the day-ahead market, the intraday market or to its own customers.

## 5. Forecasting, Scheduling and Outages

5.1 The Seller shall provide the Buyer with an hourly output forecast for the following day by 09:00 each day, an update whenever it expects a deviation of more than ten per cent (10%) of the grid export capacity in any hour, and a non-binding monthly forecast for the following twelve (12) months.

5.2 The Buyer shall act as the market representative of the Facility and shall bear all imbalance costs from the Commercial Operation Date. In that capacity the Buyer shall submit the offers and nominations for the Metered Output to the market operator and the transmission system operator, shall receive the market settlement for that output, which belongs to the Buyer, and shall be the balance responsible party for the Facility. The Seller shall grant the Buyer the necessary powers of representation and shall not revoke them while this Agreement is in force.

5.3 The Buyer remains solely responsible for its offers and for any imbalance between the offered quantity and the Metered Output. The Seller is not liable for imbalance costs except where the imbalance results from its failure to notify an outage under sections 5.4 or 5.5.

5.4 The Seller may take the Facility or part of it out of service for planned maintenance, scheduled where reasonably possible outside April to September and in hours of low irradiance. The Seller shall deliver an annual maintenance plan by 1 December for the following year and notify changes at least five (5) Business Days in advance. Planned maintenance counts as unavailability under section 5.6.

5.5 The Seller shall notify the Buyer of any unplanned outage exceeding five (5) MW within two (2) hours of becoming aware of it, stating the capacity affected and the expected duration, and shall notify the return to service.

5.6 The Seller guarantees an availability of the Facility of ninety-seven per cent (97%) in each Contract Year. Within sixty (60) days after the end of each Contract Year the Seller shall deliver an Availability statement for that year, calculated under Annex 1, with the underlying data. If Availability is below ninety-seven per cent (97%), the Seller shall pay availability damages equal to the product of (a) the shortfall in percentage points, (b) one per cent (1%) of the Expected Annual Production and (c) the Contract Price, multiplied by fifty per cent (50%). Availability damages for a Contract Year shall not exceed five per cent (5%) of the amount payable by the Buyer under section 7 for that year and are the Buyer's sole remedy for an Availability shortfall. The Buyer may have the statement verified by an independent engineer, at the Seller's cost if Availability was overstated by more than one (1) percentage point.

5.7 The Seller shall give the Buyer read-only access to real-time output, irradiance and plant status data.

## 6. Metering

6.1 The Seller shall install, at its own cost, a main meter and a check meter on the 220 kV side of the Valdeolivos substation, complying with the Spanish metering regulations and the requirements of the transmission system operator, and registered with the market operator.

6.2 The main meter is the meter of record. If it fails or is inaccurate beyond the permitted tolerance, the check meter applies. If both are unavailable, the Metered Output shall be estimated in good faith from irradiance data, inverter data and the transmission system operator's metering.

6.3 The meters shall be verified by an accredited laboratory before the Commercial Operation Date, every two (2) years thereafter and on request of either Party, which bears the cost unless an inaccuracy beyond the tolerance is found. Invoices for the period of an inaccuracy, not exceeding twelve (12) months, shall be corrected with the next invoice.

6.4 The Seller shall transfer hourly meter readings to the Buyer by 12:00 on the following day and shall give the Buyer access to the meters on reasonable notice.

## 7. Price, Invoicing and Payment

7.1 For each MWh of Metered Output delivered during the Supply Period the Buyer shall pay the Seller the Contract Price. The Contract Price is fixed for the Supply Period and is not indexed or adjusted for inflation, market prices or any other factor, except under sections 7.4 and 17.3. It includes the Guarantees of Origin and excludes value added tax.

7.2 The Seller shall invoice each calendar month within ten (10) Business Days after its end, stating the hourly Metered Output, the hours to which section 7.4 applies, the amount payable and the value added tax.

7.3 By the fifth Business Day of each month the Buyer shall provide the Seller with the hourly day-ahead prices for the Spanish bidding zone for the previous month as published by the market operator, identifying the negative hours.

7.4 In any hour in which the day-ahead price for the Spanish bidding zone is negative, the Contract Price shall not apply and no payment shall be due for electricity delivered in that hour. The Metered Output in such hours is nonetheless delivered to the Buyer and gives rise to Guarantees of Origin under section 8.

7.5 If the Buyer disputes an invoice in good faith it shall notify the Seller before the due date, stating the amount and reasons, and pay the undisputed part.

7.6 Each invoice is payable within twenty (20) days after its date of issue. Late amounts, including disputed amounts later found payable, bear interest at the Interest Rate from the due date until payment. Set-off is permitted only between amounts due and payable under this Agreement. Invoices may be corrected within twelve (12) months after issue and are then final except for fraud or manifest error.

## 8. Guarantees of Origin

8.1 All Guarantees of Origin issued in respect of the Metered Output shall be transferred to the Buyer at no additional cost. The Seller shall apply for them for all electricity delivered and instruct the issuing body to transfer them to the Buyer's register account within thirty (30) days after issue.

8.2 The Seller shall maintain the registration of the Facility with the issuing body throughout the Supply Period and shall not apply for any other certificate or support scheme which would prevent the issue of Guarantees of Origin. If Guarantees of Origin are replaced by another scheme for reasons not attributable to the Seller, the Seller shall transfer the successor certificate, without adjustment of the Contract Price.

8.3 The Buyer may cancel, trade or transfer the Guarantees of Origin at its discretion.

## 9. Credit Support

9.1 The Seller shall deliver a performance bond in the amount of EUR 1,200,000 within ten (10) Business Days after the Signing Date, to remain in force until the Commercial Operation Date. The bond shall be issued by a bank rated at least A- by a recognised rating agency, in the form in Annex 2, payable on first demand. The Buyer may draw on it for unpaid delay damages under section 3.5 and for amounts payable by the Seller under section 13 before the Commercial Operation Date, and shall return the original within ten (10) Business Days after that date.

9.2 The Buyer shall deliver a letter of credit in an amount equal to three (3) months of expected payments under this Agreement, calculated as one quarter of the Expected Annual Production multiplied by the Contract Price, being EUR 1,598,437.50 on the Signing Date. The letter of credit shall be delivered at least ten (10) Business Days before the expected Commercial Operation Date, issued by a bank rated at least A- by a recognised rating agency, in the form in Annex 3, payable on first demand, and maintained until sixty (60) days after the end of the Supply Period.

9.3 The letter of credit may have a term of one (1) year, in which case the Buyer shall deliver a replacement or extension at least thirty (30) days before expiry. Failing that, the Seller may draw the full amount and hold the proceeds as cash collateral until a replacement is delivered.

9.4 The Seller may draw on the letter of credit for any amount unpaid five (5) Business Days after written notice of non-payment, and for any Termination Amount payable by the Buyer. The Buyer shall restore the letter of credit to its full amount within ten (10) Business Days after each drawing.

9.5 If the issuing bank of either instrument ceases to hold the required rating, the Party which delivered it shall replace it within twenty (20) Business Days.

9.6 No parent company guarantee, shareholder support or other credit support is provided by either Party or by any affiliate of either Party. The Buyer confirms that it contracts as a stand-alone electricity trader and that its shareholders have given no undertaking in respect of its obligations.

## 10. Force Majeure

10.1 Force Majeure means an event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party which it could not reasonably have prevented and which prevents or delays its performance, including natural disasters, wildfires and weather beyond the design parameters in Annex 1; war, terrorism and civil disturbance; sector-wide strikes; epidemics; and failure of the transmission grid beyond the Delivery Point.

10.2 Force Majeure does not include lack of funds, a change in market prices, ordinary weather variation including low irradiance, failure of equipment caused by lack of maintenance, or the failure of a contractor or supplier unless itself caused by Force Majeure.

10.3 The affected Party shall notify the other within five (5) Business Days of becoming aware of the event, describing it, the obligations affected and the estimated duration, and shall use reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effects and resume performance.

10.4 For the duration and to the extent of the Force Majeure, the affected Party's obligations (other than to pay money and to provide credit support) are suspended. No payment is due for electricity not delivered as a result of Force Majeure.

10.5 If an event of Force Majeure continues for more than one hundred and eighty (180) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by thirty (30) days' written notice, provided the event is still continuing at the end of the notice period. No Termination Amount is payable on such termination.

## 11. Compliance and Permits

11.1 The Seller shall obtain and maintain all Permits and comply with their terms, and shall notify the Buyer promptly of any revocation, suspension or material amendment of a Permit or any proceeding which could lead to it.

11.2 The Buyer shall maintain its licence as an electricity trader and its market registrations throughout the Supply Period and shall notify the Seller promptly of any proceeding which could lead to their revocation or suspension.

11.3 Each Party shall comply with the laws applicable to it in performing this Agreement, including electricity sector legislation, market rules, grid codes and the laws on anti-corruption and sanctions.

11.4 Each Party represents on the Signing Date that it is duly incorporated, has the corporate power to enter into and perform this Agreement, and that no litigation or administrative proceeding is pending or threatened which could materially affect its performance.

11.5 The Seller shall operate the Facility in accordance with good industry practice and the Permits, through an operation and maintenance contractor experienced in utility-scale photovoltaic plants in Spain.

## 12. Events of Default

12.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default in respect of a Party (the "Defaulting Party", the other being the "Non-Defaulting Party"):

(a) failure to pay any amount due within ten (10) Business Days after written notice of non-payment;

(b) failure to deliver, maintain, replace or restore the credit support required under section 9 within ten (10) Business Days after written notice;

(c) an Insolvency Event;

(d) breach of any other material obligation not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice or, where that is not possible, within ninety (90) days after the notice;

(e) a representation in section 11.4 proves materially incorrect and the consequences are not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice;

(f) for the Seller only, sale of electricity from the Facility to a third party in breach of section 4.1, or revocation of the Permits not reinstated within ninety (90) days; and

(g) for the Buyer only, revocation or suspension of its trader licence or market registration not reinstated within thirty (30) days, or revocation of the powers of representation granted under section 5.2.

12.2 Each Party shall notify the other promptly of any Event of Default, or potential Event of Default, in respect of itself.

## 13. Termination and Termination Amount

13.1 While an Event of Default is continuing, the Non-Defaulting Party may by written notice designate an early termination date between five (5) and twenty (20) Business Days after the notice, on which this Agreement terminates. Where the Seller is the Defaulting Party, section 16.4 applies.

13.2 On early termination under section 13.1 the Defaulting Party shall pay the Non-Defaulting Party the Termination Amount, together with all amounts accrued and unpaid up to the early termination date. If the Termination Amount is negative, nothing is payable in respect of it by either Party.

13.3 The Termination Amount shall be the replacement value of this Agreement for the remainder of the Supply Period as determined by an independent expert appointed jointly by the Parties or, failing agreement on the expert within ten (10) Business Days after the early termination date, appointed on the request of either Party by the president of the arbitration institution referred to in section 20.2. The expert shall determine, under section 20.3, the present value at the Interest Rate of the difference between the Contract Price and the forward price for the Spanish bidding zone at the early termination date, evidenced by quotations from at least two (2) market participants, applied to the Expected Annual Production over the remainder of the Supply Period. The Defaulting Party bears the expert's costs.

13.4 The Termination Amount is payable within twenty (20) Business Days after the determination is delivered and may be drawn from the Defaulting Party's credit support under section 9.

13.5 This Agreement also terminates at the end of the Supply Period, under sections 2.3, 3.7 or 10.5 (in which cases no Termination Amount is payable), or by written agreement of the Parties.

13.6 Termination does not affect rights accrued before it. Sections 7, 13, 14, 18 and 20 survive.

## 14. Limitation of Liability

14.1 Neither Party is liable to the other for loss of profit, loss of revenue or any indirect or consequential loss, except that delay damages, availability damages, the Termination Amount and amounts payable under section 17 are recoverable in full.

14.2 The aggregate liability of each Party in any Contract Year, excluding the Termination Amount and any liability which cannot be limited under Spanish law, shall not exceed twenty per cent (20%) of the amount payable by the Buyer under section 7 for the Expected Annual Production at the Contract Price.

14.3 Nothing in this Agreement limits liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

## 15. Insurance

15.1 The Seller shall maintain, during construction, construction all-risks and third-party liability insurance customary for a photovoltaic plant of this size in Spain, and thereafter until the end of the Supply Period property damage insurance for the full replacement value of the Facility, business interruption insurance covering twelve (12) months of revenue, and third-party liability insurance of not less than EUR 10,000,000 per occurrence.

15.2 The Buyer shall maintain third-party liability insurance customary for an electricity trader of its size.

15.3 Each Party shall provide certificates of the required insurance on request, not more than once per Contract Year.

## 16. Assignment and Lender Protections

16.1 Neither Party may assign or transfer this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, not to be unreasonably withheld, except as provided in this section 16.

16.2 The Seller may assign its rights under this Agreement by way of security to a Lender without consent, and shall notify the Buyer. The Buyer shall, on request, enter into a direct agreement with the Lender on terms customary for project financing in Spain.

16.3 The Seller may notify the Buyer of a Lender's notice details. Until repayment of the financing is notified, the Buyer shall copy to the Lender every notice of an Event of Default or early termination which it sends to the Seller.

16.4 Where the Buyer has given notice of early termination for an Event of Default of the Seller, the Lender may, within thirty (30) days after receiving its copy, remedy the Event of Default or notify the Buyer that it intends to enforce its security, in which case the early termination date is suspended for up to one hundred and twenty (120) days, provided the Lender procures payment of amounts falling due to the Buyer meanwhile.

16.5 The Seller may transfer this Agreement together with the Facility, without consent, to a purchaser which, directly or through its contractor, has at least three (3) years of experience operating photovoltaic plants of not less than 200 MW in aggregate and which assumes the Seller's obligations in writing.

16.6 The Buyer may transfer this Agreement to another licensed electricity trader with the Seller's prior written consent, which may be withheld if the transferee does not first deliver a letter of credit meeting section 9.2.

16.7 A change of control of the Seller is not an assignment, provided the Seller notifies the Buyer within ten (10) Business Days after it occurs.

## 17. Taxes, Levies and Regulatory Costs

17.1 The Seller bears all taxes, levies, charges and fees imposed on the generation of electricity and on the Facility, including the tax on the value of electricity production, grid access charges payable by generators and local taxes on the site. The Buyer bears all taxes, levies, charges and fees imposed on the purchase, trading or supply of electricity from the Delivery Point onward, including the charges of the market operator and the transmission system operator.

17.2 Value added tax is payable in addition to the Contract Price at the applicable rate. Each Party shall provide the documentation reasonably required to apply an exemption, reverse charge or reduced rate.

17.3 Should any law, regulation or administrative decision enacted after the Signing Date impose a new tax, levy or charge on the generation or sale of electricity from the Facility, the Seller shall bear such cost unless it exceeds three per cent (3%) of the Seller's revenue under this Agreement in the relevant Contract Year, in which case the Parties shall meet to agree an equitable adjustment. The Seller shall notify the Buyer of the new tax, levy or charge, with a calculation of its expected cost, within thirty (30) days after enactment, and the Parties shall meet within thirty (30) days after the notice. Any adjustment is limited to the portion of the cost above the three per cent (3%) threshold. If no adjustment is agreed within ninety (90) days, either Party may refer the matter to the expert procedure in section 20.3.

17.4 Should any such law, regulation or administrative decision abolish or reduce a tax, levy or charge on the generation or sale of electricity from the Facility, the benefit remains with the Seller. Should it impose a new tax, levy or charge on the purchase, trading or supply of electricity by the Buyer, the Buyer bears that cost in full.

17.5 No adjustment is available under section 17.3 for corporate income tax or for any penalty or surcharge arising from the Seller's non-compliance.

20.3 Matters which this Agreement refers to an expert (sections 5.6, 6, 13.3 and 17.3) shall be determined by an independent expert appointed jointly by the Parties or, failing agreement within ten (10) Business Days, by the president of the arbitration institution referred to in section 20.2. # Annex 1 — Facility Description

A1.1 Name: Valdeolivos Solar Park. Location: municipality of Valdeolivos, Province of Badajoz, Extremadura, Spain. Site area approximately 185 hectares, held under surface rights outlasting the Supply Period by at least five (5) years.

A1.2 Technology: ground-mounted solar photovoltaic plant with bifacial crystalline silicon modules on single-axis trackers, string inverters and medium-voltage transformer stations.

A1.3 Installed capacity: 90 MWp (direct current), approximately 150,000 modules of 600 Wp. Grid export capacity: 75 MW (alternating current), limited at the inverters in accordance with the access and connection permits.

A1.4 Connection: 30 kV internal network to the Valdeolivos substation, where a 30/220 kV transformer of 80 MVA steps up to 220 kV. The Delivery Point is the 220 kV connection point at the Valdeolivos substation, at the boundary with the transmission system operator's busbar.

A1.5 Expected Annual Production: 165,000 MWh (P50), as assessed by an independent technical adviser from long-term irradiance data, a performance ratio of eighty-two per cent (82%) and the export limitation at 75 MW; not adjusted for module degradation.

A1.6 Availability method. For each Daylight Hour the Facility counts as available if the capacity of the inverters in service, adjusted for the irradiance measured on site, equals at least ninety-five per cent (95%) of the output the Facility would have produced in that hour with all inverters in service. Excluded hours are removed from both numerator and denominator. The Seller shall keep the underlying data for three (3) years.

A1.7 Design parameters for section 10.1: wind up to 140 km/h; hail up to 25 mm; ambient temperature from minus 10 to plus 45 degrees Celsius; hundred-year flood level.
