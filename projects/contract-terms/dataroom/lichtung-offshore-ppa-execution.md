# Part I — Individual Terms

The elections in this Part I form part of the General Provisions in Part II. Capitalised terms have the meanings given in section 1 of Part II. Where Part I conflicts with Part II, Part I prevails.

## Parties

Seller: Lichtung Offshore Wind GmbH & Co. KG, a limited partnership organised under the laws of Germany with its seat in Emden, acting through its general partner Lichtung Offshore Wind Verwaltungs GmbH. The Seller is a single-purpose project company for the Facility.

Buyer: Rheintal Spezialchemie AG, a stock corporation organised under the laws of Germany with its seat in Worms. On the Signing Date the Buyer holds a long-term credit rating of BBB+ from a recognised rating agency. The Buyer is an industrial electricity consumer with production sites in the DE-LU bidding zone.

Seller's Credit Support Provider: Lichtung Energie Holding GmbH, Hamburg, the ultimate parent of the Seller.

## Facility

The Facility is an offshore wind farm with an installed capacity of 300 MW located in the German North Sea approximately 60 km north-west of Borkum, consisting of twenty (20) wind turbine generators of 15 MW each on monopile foundations, one offshore substation and the inter-array cables, connected to the onshore transmission grid through the offshore grid connection system of the transmission system operator. Annex 1 describes the Facility in more detail.

## Supply Period

Supply Period: fifteen (15) years commencing on the Start Date of 1 January 2029. The Supply Period ends on 31 December 2043 whether or not the Facility is commissioned on the Start Date and is not extended by any delay in commissioning.

## Product and Certificates

Product: (a) the financial settlement of the Contract Quantity against the Reference Price under section 4, and (b) the transfer to the Buyer of Guarantees of Origin in a quantity equal to the Contract Quantity under section 8. No electricity is sold, purchased or delivered under this Agreement.

## Contract Quantity

Contract Quantity: 1,000,000 MWh per Contract Year, delivered as a baseload profile in equal hourly quantities. The Hourly Quantity for any hour is the Contract Quantity divided by the number of hours in the Contract Year in which that hour falls (114.155 MWh in a Contract Year of 8,760 hours; 113.843 MWh in a Contract Year of 8,784 hours). The Contract Quantity does not vary with the actual output of the Facility. For the first Contract Year only, it is reduced under section 4.5 if the Commissioning Date falls after the Start Date.

## Price

Strike Price: EUR 72.00 per MWh, increased by two per cent (2%) on 1 January of each Contract Year from the second Contract Year onwards. The Strike Price is stated here and in section 7.1 and is not contained in any election sheet or annex. Reference Price: as defined in section 1.1.

## Settlement

Settlement: financial. No physical delivery of electricity takes place under this Agreement. For each hour of the Supply Period from the Commissioning Date, the Parties settle the difference between the Strike Price and the Reference Price on the Hourly Quantity in accordance with section 4.

## Delivery Point and Balancing

Delivery Point: not applicable. The Seller sells the physical output of the Facility in the market for its own account and remains responsible for the balancing of the Facility (section 5.2). The Buyer procures its physical electricity separately and balances its own consumption.

## Credit Support

Seller: parent company guarantee from Lichtung Energie Holding GmbH in the amount of EUR 25,000,000 in the form of Annex 4 (section 9.1).

Buyer: no credit support on the Signing Date. A letter of credit of EUR 20,000,000 in the form of Annex 3 becomes due if the rating trigger in section 9.3 occurs.

## Governing Law

German law; arbitration seated in Hamburg (section 20).

# Part II — General Provisions

## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement the following capitalised terms have the meanings given below. Terms defined in Part I or in a later section have the meaning given there.

Business Day means a day other than a Saturday, a Sunday or a public holiday on which banks are open for general business in Frankfurt am Main.

Commissioning Date means the date on which the Facility is commissioned in accordance with section 3.2.

Contract Year means each calendar year within the Supply Period, the first beginning on the Start Date and the last ending on 31 December 2043.

Financial Close means the date on which the senior debt financing agreements for the Facility have been signed by the Seller and the Lenders and all conditions to the first drawdown under them have been satisfied or waived.

Guarantee of Origin means an electronic certificate issued under the German guarantee of origin legislation by the competent registry, evidencing that one MWh of electricity was generated from a renewable energy source, and any successor instrument.

Installed Capacity means 300 MW.

Lenders means the financial institutions providing debt financing to the Seller for the Facility, and their agents.

Longstop Commissioning Date means 1 July 2029.

Reference Price means the hourly day-ahead price for the DE-LU bidding zone, expressed in EUR per MWh, as published by the day-ahead market operator for the hour in question. Where the day-ahead market operator publishes prices for a market time unit shorter than one hour, the Reference Price for an hour is the arithmetic average of the prices for the market time units within that hour. Where section 11 applies, Reference Price means the Replacement Reference Price from the date on which it takes effect.

Reference Price Source means the day-ahead auction for the DE-LU bidding zone operated by the day-ahead market operator, from which the Reference Price is taken.

Signing Date means 3 November 2025.

Start Date means 1 January 2029.

Termination Amount means the amount calculated in accordance with Annex 5 (Termination Amount Methodology) and payable under section 13.

Transmission System Operator means the operator of the transmission system to which the Facility is connected, including the operator of the offshore grid connection system serving the Facility.

## 3. Construction, Milestones and Commissioning

3.1 The Seller shall develop, construct and commission the Facility at its own cost and risk in accordance with good industry practice, its permits and applicable law, and shall use reasonable endeavours to achieve the following milestones: (a) Financial Close by 30 June 2026; (b) start of offshore installation works by 31 March 2028; (c) first export of electricity from the Facility by 30 September 2028; and (d) the Commissioning Date on or before the Start Date. Until the Commissioning Date the Seller shall report progress quarterly.

3.2 The Facility is commissioned when wind turbine generators with an aggregate capacity of at least 240 MW (eighty per cent (80%) of the Installed Capacity) have passed their take-over tests under the turbine supply contracts, are connected to the offshore grid connection system and are capable of exporting electricity to the transmission grid. The Seller shall certify this in writing to the Buyer with the Transmission System Operator's confirmation of the connection attached; the Commissioning Date is the date stated in that certificate.

3.3 If the Facility has not been commissioned by the Start Date, the Seller shall pay the Buyer delay compensation of EUR 40,000 for each day from the Start Date until the Facility is commissioned, up to an aggregate cap of EUR 6,000,000 (Delay Compensation). Delay Compensation is invoiced monthly in arrears under section 7.3 and is the Buyer's sole financial remedy for late commissioning, without prejudice to section 3.4. Its accrual is suspended while the delay is caused by Force Majeure, but the Longstop Commissioning Date is not extended by Force Majeure.

3.4 If the Facility has not been commissioned by 1 July 2029, the Buyer may terminate this Agreement by written notice without any Termination Amount being payable by either Party. This right may be exercised at any time after the Longstop Commissioning Date until the Commissioning Date occurs. Delay Compensation accrued up to the date of termination remains payable.

## 4. Settlement

4.1 For each hour of the Supply Period from and including the later of the Start Date and 00:00 on the day following the Commissioning Date, the Hourly Quantity is settled between the Parties under this section 4. The Contract Quantity is settled regardless of whether, and how much, electricity the Facility generates in the hour concerned.

4.2 The Settlement Amount for an hour is the Strike Price for the relevant Contract Year minus the Reference Price for that hour, multiplied by the Hourly Quantity. If the Settlement Amount is positive, the Buyer owes it to the Seller; if negative, the Seller owes its absolute value to the Buyer.

4.3 The Settlement Amounts for all hours of a calendar month are summed to produce the Monthly Settlement Amount, which is invoiced and paid net under section 7 by the Buyer if positive and by the Seller if negative.

4.4 An hour for which the Reference Price is below zero is a Negative Price Hour. No Settlement Amount is payable by either Party for a Negative Price Hour. Negative Price Hours do not reduce the quantity of Guarantees of Origin due under section 8.

4.5 If the Commissioning Date falls after the Start Date, the Contract Quantity for the first Contract Year is reduced by the Hourly Quantity multiplied by the number of hours from the Start Date until settlement begins under section 4.1. Delay Compensation is the only amount payable for those hours.

4.6 The Seller is free to sell the physical output of the Facility to any person, and shall do so without claiming any statutory market premium, so that Guarantees of Origin may be issued. The Buyer acquires no title to any electricity generated by the Facility. Annex 2 illustrates this section 4.

## 5. Forecasting, Scheduling and Outages

5.1 Not later than 1 December before each Contract Year the Seller shall provide a non-binding forecast of the expected annual production of the Facility and of the planned maintenance periods, for information only.

5.2 The Seller shall remain responsible for the balancing of the Facility and shall bear all imbalance costs. The Seller shall at all times appoint a balance responsible party for the Facility. The Buyer is responsible for the balancing of its own consumption.

5.3 The Seller shall notify the Buyer of any outage that reduces the available capacity of the Facility by more than 50 MW for more than seven (7) consecutive days. Outages do not reduce the Contract Quantity.

5.4 The Seller's obligations in respect of the Contract Quantity shall not be reduced by any curtailment of the Facility's output instructed by the transmission system operator, save where such curtailment constitutes Force Majeure. Curtailment for congestion management, redispatch, system security or market-related reasons is not Force Majeure. Compensation paid by the Transmission System Operator for curtailment belongs to the Seller.

5.5 The Seller gives no guarantee of availability or production.

## 6. Metering

6.1 The electricity exported by the Facility is metered at the Transmission System Operator's metering point on the offshore grid connection system. The Seller shall ensure that the metering data is made available to the competent registry for the issue of Guarantees of Origin, and shall provide the Buyer with the metered export by hour within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month, for the purposes of section 8.

6.2 Because settlement is financial, metering data does not affect any Settlement Amount. A metering error affects only the Guarantees of Origin that may be issued.

## 7. Price, Invoicing and Payment

7.1 The Strike Price for the first Contract Year (2029) is EUR 72.00 per MWh. On 1 January of each Contract Year from the second Contract Year onwards the Strike Price is increased by two per cent (2%), each year's Strike Price being the previous year's Strike Price multiplied by 1.02 and rounded to two decimal places: EUR 73.44 per MWh in 2030, EUR 74.91 in 2031, EUR 76.41 in 2032, EUR 77.94 in 2033, and so on. The Strike Price includes the consideration for the Guarantees of Origin.

7.2 Within five (5) Business Days after the end of each calendar month the Seller shall deliver a settlement statement showing, for each hour, the Hourly Quantity, the Reference Price, the Settlement Amount and whether the hour was a Negative Price Hour, together with the Monthly Settlement Amount.

7.3 The Party to which the Monthly Settlement Amount is owed shall invoice it within three (3) Business Days after the settlement statement. Other amounts are invoiced by the Party entitled to them. Each invoice is payable in euro within ten (10) Business Days after receipt.

7.4 A Party disputing an invoice shall pay the undisputed part when due and notify the disputed part within twenty (20) Business Days. Overdue amounts bear interest at the statutory default interest rate for commercial transactions under German law.

7.5 All amounts are exclusive of value added tax. Each Party may set off amounts due to it under this Agreement against amounts it owes under this Agreement; no other set-off or withholding is permitted, save as required by law.

## 8. Guarantees of Origin

8.1 The Seller shall transfer to the Buyer Guarantees of Origin in a quantity equal to the Contract Quantity in each Contract Year. The Guarantees of Origin shall be issued for electricity generated by the Facility, carry the technology attribute "offshore wind", and be transferred to the Buyer's registry account within forty-five (45) days after the end of each calendar quarter, with any balance for the Contract Year transferred within ninety (90) days after the end of the Contract Year.

8.2 If the electricity generated by the Facility in a Contract Year is less than the Contract Quantity, the Seller shall procure Guarantees of Origin for the shortfall from other offshore wind facilities in Germany with a production period in the same Contract Year, at its own cost.

8.3 If the Seller fails to transfer the full quantity for a Contract Year within ninety (90) days after its end, the Buyer may procure substitute Guarantees of Origin of a similar description and the Seller shall reimburse the documented cost within ten (10) Business Days after invoice. This is the Buyer's sole remedy for a shortfall, without prejudice to section 12.1(g).

8.4 Each Party bears its own registry fees. The Seller bears issuing fees; the Buyer bears cancellation fees and may use or transfer the Guarantees of Origin for any lawful purpose.

Credit support reduced by a drawing shall be restored within ten (10) Business Days, and credit support no longer required shall be returned within twenty (20) Business Days after the later of the end of the Supply Period and the payment of all amounts due.

## 10. Force Majeure

10.1 Force Majeure means an event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party that it could not reasonably have prevented or overcome and that prevents or delays the performance of its obligations, including: war, terrorism or sabotage; storm, sea state or ice conditions exceeding the design parameters of the Facility; fire, explosion or flood; nation-wide strikes; the physical failure or destruction of the Transmission System Operator's offshore grid connection system or export cable not caused by the Seller; and an instruction of the Transmission System Operator to disconnect or curtail the Facility that results from an event which itself qualifies as Force Majeure.

10.2 Force Majeure does not include changes in market prices; lack of funds; a failure of a contractor or supplier unless itself caused by Force Majeure; ordinary weather, including low wind; curtailment for congestion management, redispatch, system security or market-related reasons; or an event that could have been avoided by compliance with good industry practice.

10.3 A Party affected by Force Majeure is relieved from the affected obligations for as long as, and to the extent that, the Force Majeure prevents performance, provided that it notifies the other Party within five (5) Business Days, and uses reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effects. Payment obligations for amounts already due are not suspended.

10.4 Where Force Majeure affecting the Seller renders part or all of the Installed Capacity unavailable during the Supply Period, the Hourly Quantity for each affected hour is reduced in the proportion that the unavailable capacity bears to the Installed Capacity, and the obligation under section 8 is reduced accordingly. No other adjustment is made for Force Majeure, and neither Party is liable for losses caused by it.

10.5 If an event of Force Majeure continues for more than one hundred and eighty (180) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice with effect from a date not less than ten (10) Business Days after the notice. No Termination Amount is payable on such a termination; amounts accrued before termination remain payable.

## 11. Change in Law and Change in Reference Price Source

11.1 Change in Law means the enactment, amendment, repeal or replacement after the Signing Date of any law, regulation or binding decision of a public authority of Germany or the European Union, or a change after the Signing Date in their binding interpretation by a court or public authority, that affects the Product, the Reference Price Source, the Guarantees of Origin or the rights and obligations of a Party. It excludes a change in taxes on income.

11.2 If a Change in Law results in the Product, or any material part of it, no longer existing or no longer being capable of being delivered, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice without any Termination Amount being payable. Before exercising this right the Parties shall negotiate in good faith for ninety (90) days from the notice of the Change in Law with a view to amending this Agreement so as to preserve its economic effect for both Parties; if they agree an amendment within that period, the termination right lapses.

11.3 Any other Change in Law, including one that changes the cost of Guarantees of Origin, grid charges or levies, is borne by the Party on whom the cost or benefit falls. No adjustment of the Strike Price or the Contract Quantity is made; section 17 applies to taxes and levies.

11.4 Change in Reference Price Source means any of the following events after the Signing Date: (a) the day-ahead market operator ceases to publish an hourly day-ahead price for the DE-LU bidding zone; (b) the DE-LU bidding zone is split or reconfigured so that no single day-ahead price is published for the whole area in which the Facility and the Buyer's production sites are located; (c) the day-ahead market operator materially changes the methodology, currency or auction design by which the Reference Price is determined; or (d) the day-ahead market operator ceases to be a nominated operator of the day-ahead market for the DE-LU bidding zone and no successor publishes a comparable price.

11.5 Either Party may notify the other of a Change in Reference Price Source. Within sixty (60) days after that notice the Parties shall negotiate in good faith and agree a replacement price (the Replacement Reference Price) that reproduces as closely as reasonably possible the economic effect of the Reference Price before the Change in Reference Price Source. If no Replacement Reference Price has been agreed within the sixty (60) days, either Party may refer the determination to an independent expert with at least ten (10) years' experience of European wholesale electricity markets, appointed by agreement or, failing agreement within ten (10) Business Days, at the request of either Party by a recognised arbitral institution in Germany. The expert acts as an expert and not as an arbitrator and shall deliver a reasoned determination within thirty (30) days after appointment, binding save in the case of manifest error or fraud. The costs of the expert are borne equally.

11.6 Until a Replacement Reference Price takes effect, the Parties settle provisionally on the price that in the Seller's reasonable opinion most closely corresponds to the former Reference Price, and true up with interest once the Replacement Reference Price is agreed or determined. The Replacement Reference Price takes effect from the date on which the Change in Reference Price Source first affected the published price.

11.7 A Change in Reference Price Source gives neither Party a right to terminate this Agreement or to adjust the Strike Price.

## 12. Events of Default

12.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default in relation to a Party (the Defaulting Party, the other being the Non-Defaulting Party):

(a) the Party fails to pay any amount due and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice;

(b) the Party fails to deliver, maintain, renew or restore credit support under section 9 and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice;

(c) the Party or its credit support provider becomes insolvent or is the subject of an application for the opening of insolvency proceedings which is not dismissed within thirty (30) days;

(d) the Party fails to perform any other material obligation and the failure, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within thirty (30) days after written notice;

(e) the Party repudiates this Agreement;

(f) in relation to the Seller, the Seller abandons the construction or operation of the Facility for ninety (90) consecutive days other than by reason of Force Majeure;

(g) in relation to the Seller, the Seller fails to transfer the full quantity of Guarantees of Origin for a Contract Year within one hundred and twenty (120) days after its end; or

(h) the Party assigns this Agreement, or undergoes a change of control, in breach of section 16.

## 13. Termination and Termination Amount

13.1 While an Event of Default continues, the Non-Defaulting Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the Defaulting Party designating a date (the Early Termination Date) not earlier than five (5) and not later than twenty (20) Business Days after the notice, identifying the Event of Default relied on. A termination notice given by the Buyer is subject to the Lenders' rights under section 16.2.

13.2 On the Early Termination Date all obligations under sections 4, 7 and 8 cease for later hours, and all amounts accrued but unpaid become due and payable, without prejudice to the Termination Amount.

13.4 A dispute over the statement is referred to an independent expert appointed and acting as in section 11.5; the undisputed part is paid when due.

13.5 No Termination Amount is payable on a termination under section 2.2, 3.4, 10.5 or 11.2. On any such termination, amounts accrued before termination, including Delay Compensation and costs under section 8.3, remain payable.

## Annex 1 — Description of the Facility

1. Name: Lichtung Offshore Wind Farm.

2. Location: German Exclusive Economic Zone, North Sea, approximately 60 km north-west of Borkum, centred on approximately 54.0° N, 6.0° E; water depth 30 to 35 metres. The site was awarded to the Seller in a competitive tender in 2024 without a statutory market premium.
