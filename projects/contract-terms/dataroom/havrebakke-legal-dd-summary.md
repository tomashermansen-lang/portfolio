# Legal due diligence summary — Havrebakke Wind Farm PPA

Synthetic document, written to demonstrate a method. Fictional parties and figures. Not a real contract and not legal advice.

**Prepared by:** Søbredden Advokatpartnerselskab (fictional)
**For:** the investment team, Project Havrebakke
**Document reviewed:** Power Purchase Agreement between Havrebakke Vind ApS (Seller) and Jutland Board & Packaging A/S (Buyer), execution version dated 28 May 2026
**Date of this summary:** 4 June 2026
**Scope:** key commercial terms and bankability points only. Title, permits, grid connection and construction contracts are covered in separate memoranda.

## 1. Summary view

The agreement is a pay-as-produced physical PPA under Danish law with an industrial offtaker whose obligations are guaranteed by its parent. The commercial terms are within the range we see for Danish onshore wind contracted to industrial buyers. Three points deserve the committee's attention: the Buyer is unrated and the parent guarantee is the credit; the Buyer holds a curtailment right of 150 hours per year, compensated; and a change-in-law clause gives either party a termination right after a failed renegotiation, without a termination amount.

## 3. Points for the committee

1. **Credit.** The Buyer is unrated. The parent company guarantee in Annex 4 is the credit the committee is relying on; we recommend the financial team reviews the guarantor's accounts for the last three years.
2. **Curtailment right.** A 150-hour Buyer curtailment right is at the upper end of what we see. It is compensated at the Contract Price, so the revenue effect is neutral, but the committee should note that the Seller has no equivalent right.
3. **Change in law.** The termination right after a failed renegotiation is mutual and carries no Termination Amount. Lenders will ask for this to be tightened; we expect a lender consent letter to be required at Financial Close.
4. **Termination Amount.** The mark-to-market formula references a "Reference Forward Price" defined by the Parties' agreement on a source. The source is named in the agreement; we have confirmed it is a published forward curve for DK1.
