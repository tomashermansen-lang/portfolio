# Part I — Individual Terms

## Parties

Seller: Havrebakke Vind ApS, a private limited company incorporated in Denmark, with its registered office in Ringkøbing-Skjern Municipality, Denmark.

Buyer: Jutland Board & Packaging A/S, a public limited company incorporated in Denmark, operating a corrugated board and packaging plant in Jutland. The Buyer does not hold a credit rating from a recognised rating agency.

Guarantor: Jutland Industri Holding A/S. The Buyer's payment obligations under this Agreement are guaranteed by Jutland Industri Holding A/S under the Parent Company Guarantee set out in Annex 4.

## Facility

The Facility is the Havrebakke Wind Farm, consisting of eight onshore wind turbine generators with a total installed capacity of 48.0 MW located at Havrebakke in Ringkøbing-Skjern Municipality, Denmark, in bidding zone DK1, together with the internal cabling, the Havrebakke substation and the ancillary installations described in Annex 1.

## Supply Period

Supply Period: twelve (12) years commencing on the Commercial Operation Date.

Target Commercial Operation Date: 1 October 2027.

Longstop Date: 31 March 2028.

## Product and Certificates

Product: electricity generated by the Facility and delivered at the Delivery Point, together with all Guarantees of Origin issued in respect of the Metered Output. No capacity product, ancillary service or other attribute is sold under this Agreement.

## Contract Quantity and Volume Structure

Volume structure: pay-as-produced. The Buyer shall purchase one hundred per cent (100%) of the Metered Output of the Facility during the Supply Period.

Expected Annual Production: 158,000 MWh (P50).

Minimum Annual Production: 118,500 MWh.

## Price

The Contract Price is the price per MWh set out in Annex 2 (Election Sheet). Any adjustment of the Contract Price during the Supply Period is governed exclusively by Annex 2.

## Settlement

Settlement: physical. Title to and risk in the electricity pass from the Seller to the Buyer at the Delivery Point.

## Delivery Point and Balancing

Delivery Point: the metering point on the 60 kV side of the Havrebakke substation.

Balancing: the Buyer is responsible for balancing. The Buyer shall appoint the balance responsible party for the Facility and shall bear all imbalance costs from the Commercial Operation Date.

## Credit Support

Seller: a bank guarantee of EUR 2,000,000 in the form set out in Annex 3, as described in section 9.1.

Buyer: the Parent Company Guarantee in Annex 4 and a letter of credit in the form set out in Annex 3, as described in section 9.2.

## Governing Law

Danish law. Disputes are resolved by arbitration seated in Copenhagen, as set out in section 20.

## Annexes

Annex 1 — Facility Description; Annex 2 — Election Sheet; Annex 3 — Form of Bank Guarantee and Form of Letter of Credit; Annex 4 — Parent Company Guarantee. All Annexes form part of this Agreement.

Contract Year means the period from the Commercial Operation Date to the following 31 December, each subsequent calendar year, and the period from the last 1 January in the Supply Period to the last day of the Supply Period.

Deemed Delivered Electricity means the quantity of electricity, expressed in MWh, that the Facility would have generated during a period of reduced output instructed by the Buyer under section 5.4, calculated in accordance with section 5.6.

Defaulting Party and Non-Defaulting Party have the meanings given in section 12.1.

Expected Annual Production and Minimum Annual Production mean the quantities set out in Part I under the heading Contract Quantity and Volume Structure.

Financial Close means the date on which the Seller's financing agreements for the construction of the Facility have become unconditional and the first drawdown under them has been made.

(a) the Seller having obtained the construction permit and the environmental approval for the Facility in final form;

(b) the Seller having entered into a grid connection agreement for the Facility;

(c) the Seller having reached Financial Close;

(d) the Buyer having delivered the Parent Company Guarantee, duly executed by the Guarantor, to the Seller; and

(e) each Party having delivered to the other its corporate approvals for this Agreement.

2.2 Each Party shall use reasonable endeavours to satisfy the Conditions Precedent within its control and shall notify the other Party as each is satisfied.

2.3 If all Conditions Precedent have not been satisfied or waived by 31 December 2026, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party, without liability of either Party except for a breach that occurred before termination.

2.4 Sections 1, 2, 18, 19, 20 and 21 are binding from the Signing Date.

## 3. Construction, Milestones and Commercial Operation

3.1 The Seller shall design, procure, construct, commission and test the Facility at its own cost and risk, in accordance with good industry practice, the permits and the grid connection agreement.

3.2 The Seller shall report to the Buyer on construction progress against the schedule in Annex 1 within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar quarter from Financial Close until the Commercial Operation Date.

3.3 The Commercial Operation Date occurs when all eight wind turbine generators have passed their commissioning tests, the Facility is connected to the grid at the Delivery Point with the meters installed and verified, the Facility is registered with the Transmission System Operator and the Danish issuing body for Guarantees of Origin, and the Seller has given the Buyer written notice confirming this. The Seller shall give the Buyer at least thirty (30) days' notice of the expected Commercial Operation Date.

3.4 If the Commercial Operation Date has not occurred by the Target Commercial Operation Date of 1 October 2027, the Seller shall pay the Buyer liquidated damages of EUR 9,600 for each day of delay, up to an aggregate cap of EUR 1,750,000. The liquidated damages are the Buyer's sole remedy for delay, except for the right to terminate under section 3.6.

3.5 The Target Commercial Operation Date and the Longstop Date are extended day for day for any delay caused by Force Majeure or by a breach by the Buyer of this Agreement, up to a maximum of one hundred and eighty (180) days in aggregate.

3.6 If the Commercial Operation Date has not occurred by the Longstop Date, as extended under section 3.5, the Buyer may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the Seller. On such termination the Seller shall pay any liquidated damages accrued under section 3.4 and unpaid, and no Termination Amount is payable by either Party.

## 4. Delivery and Acceptance

4.1 The Seller shall sell and deliver, and the Buyer shall purchase and accept, the Metered Output at the Delivery Point throughout the Supply Period. The Buyer shall purchase one hundred per cent (100%) of the Metered Output of the Facility during the Supply Period. The Seller shall not sell electricity generated by the Facility to any other person during the Supply Period.

4.2 Delivery takes place at the Delivery Point, where title to and risk in the electricity pass to the Buyer, who is responsible for its offtake, transport, sale and use from that point.

4.3 The Seller does not guarantee any particular quantity of electricity in any hour, day or month; the Expected Annual Production is a P50 estimate used for sections 9.2 and 13.3 only.

4.4 The Seller shall operate and maintain the Facility in accordance with good industry practice so as to maximise the Metered Output.

4.5 If in any Contract Year the sum of the Metered Output and the Deemed Delivered Electricity is less than the Minimum Annual Production, the Seller shall pay the Buyer a shortfall payment equal to the shortfall in MWh multiplied by the amount, if positive, by which the average day-ahead price for bidding zone DK1 for that Contract Year exceeds the Contract Price, capped at EUR 500,000 per Contract Year. For the first and last Contract Year the Minimum Annual Production is reduced in proportion to the number of days in that Contract Year.

4.6 For the purposes of section 4.5 the Minimum Annual Production is further reduced by the electricity the Facility could not generate because of Force Majeure, reductions of output instructed by the Transmission System Operator or a breach by the Buyer, calculated under section 5.6.

## 5. Forecasting, Scheduling and Outages

5.1 From the Commercial Operation Date the Seller shall provide the balance responsible party with a production forecast for each hour of the following day by 09:00 Danish time and shall update it intraday whenever the expected output changes by more than 10 MW. The forecasts are given with reasonable care but without warranty.

5.2 The Buyer shall appoint the balance responsible party for the Facility and shall bear all imbalance costs from the Commercial Operation Date. The balance responsible party shall be registered for the Facility no later than thirty (30) days before the expected Commercial Operation Date, and the Seller shall provide it with the operational data it reasonably requests.

5.3 The Seller shall notify the Buyer of planned maintenance at least thirty (30) days in advance, scheduling it as far as practicable in periods of low wind, and of any unplanned outage affecting more than one turbine generator without undue delay.

5.4 The Buyer may instruct the Seller to reduce the output of the Facility for up to one hundred and fifty (150) hours per Contract Year, and the electricity that would otherwise have been generated during such hours shall be treated as Deemed Delivered Electricity and paid at the Contract Price. Each instruction shall be given at least two (2) hours in advance, stating the start time, duration and output level, and the Seller may refuse any instruction beyond the limit in this section.

5.5 Reductions of output instructed by the transmission system operator shall not give rise to any payment by the Buyer. Any compensation paid by the Transmission System Operator for such a reduction belongs to the Seller.

5.6 Deemed Delivered Electricity and any other deemed quantity under this Agreement are calculated from the wind measured at the Facility's meteorological mast, the turbine supplier's power curve, the availability of each turbine generator at the time and the electrical losses to the Delivery Point. The Seller shall provide the calculation with the invoice for the relevant month.

## 6. Metering

6.1 The Metering Equipment consists of a main meter and a check meter at the Delivery Point, owned by the Seller and compliant with the metering regulations applicable in Denmark. The readings of the main meter, as registered by the grid operator, are the basis for invoicing.

6.2 The Seller shall have the meters tested by an accredited body at least every three (3) years and otherwise at the Buyer's request and cost. If a meter is inaccurate beyond the permitted tolerance, the Seller shall repair or replace it and the readings since the last test, or for six (6) months, whichever is shorter, shall be corrected by reference to the check meter.

6.3 The Seller shall give the Buyer access to the hourly metering data for the Facility.

## 7. Price, Invoicing and Payment

7.1 The Contract Price is the price per MWh set out in Annex 2 (Election Sheet). The Contract Price applies to each MWh of Metered Output and to each MWh of Deemed Delivered Electricity. Any adjustment of the Contract Price during the Supply Period is made only as provided in Annex 2.

7.2 Within ten (10) Business Days after the end of each calendar month the Seller shall invoice the Buyer for the Metered Output and the Deemed Delivered Electricity of that month, stating the quantities in MWh, the Contract Price, the amount payable and value added tax.

7.3 The Buyer shall pay each invoice within twenty (20) days after receipt to the bank account stated in the invoice.

7.4 Any other amount payable under this Agreement shall be invoiced separately and paid within twenty (20) days after receipt of the invoice.

7.5 A Party that disputes an invoice in good faith shall pay the undisputed part by the due date and state its reasons within ten (10) Business Days after receipt. Any amount later found to be due bears interest under section 7.6 from the original due date.

7.6 Overdue amounts bear interest from the due date to the date of payment at the reference rate of the Danish central bank plus eight (8) percentage points per annum.

## 8. Guarantees of Origin

8.1 All Guarantees of Origin issued in respect of the Metered Output shall be transferred to the Buyer at no additional cost. The Seller shall register the Facility with the Danish issuing body before the Commercial Operation Date and shall transfer the Guarantees of Origin for each calendar month to the Buyer's registry account within fifteen (15) Business Days after their issue.

8.2 Guarantees of Origin are not issued for Deemed Delivered Electricity, and the Seller is not obliged to procure replacement Guarantees of Origin for any shortfall against the Expected Annual Production.

8.3 If the Guarantee of Origin scheme is replaced by another scheme evidencing the renewable origin of electricity, the Seller shall transfer to the Buyer the certificates issued under the replacement scheme in respect of the Metered Output, at no additional cost.

## 9. Credit Support

9.1 The Seller shall deliver a bank guarantee in the amount of EUR 2,000,000 within ten (10) Business Days after Financial Close, to remain in force until twelve (12) months after the Commercial Operation Date. The Bank Guarantee shall be issued by a bank with a long-term credit rating of at least A- or equivalent from a recognised rating agency, in the form set out in Annex 3. The Buyer may draw on it for any amount unpaid by the Seller under sections 3.4, 3.6 or 4.5 after ten (10) Business Days' written notice.

The Buyer shall deliver a letter of credit in an amount equal to nine (9) months of expected payments under this Agreement, calculated as nine twelfths of the Expected Annual Production multiplied by the Contract Price in force at the time of calculation, no later than thirty (30) days before the expected Commercial Operation Date, to remain in force until sixty (60) days after the end of the Supply Period. The Letter of Credit shall be issued by a bank with a long-term credit rating of at least A- or equivalent from a recognised rating agency, in the form set out in Annex 3, and its amount is recalculated on 1 January of each Contract Year to reflect the Contract Price then in force.

9.4 If the issuing bank of the Bank Guarantee or the Letter of Credit loses the required rating, or if the instrument is not extended at least thirty (30) days before an expiry falling before the required date, the Party that delivered it shall replace it within twenty (20) Business Days, failing which the other Party may draw the full amount as cash security.

## 10. Force Majeure

10.1 Force Majeure means an event beyond the reasonable control of the affected Party that it could not reasonably have prevented or overcome and that prevents or delays the performance of its obligations under this Agreement, including natural disaster, storm beyond the design parameters of the Facility, fire, war, terrorism, epidemic, strike other than one limited to the affected Party's own workforce, and failure of the grid beyond the Delivery Point. Force Majeure does not include lack of funds, a change in market prices or insufficient wind.

10.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party within five (5) Business Days, stating the event, its expected duration and the obligations affected, and shall use reasonable endeavours to mitigate its effects.

10.3 The obligations of the affected Party, other than payment of amounts already due, are suspended for as long as the Force Majeure prevents their performance. No payment for undelivered electricity, shortfall payment or liquidated damages accrues for the period of Force Majeure.

10.4 If an event of Force Majeure continues for more than one hundred and eighty (180) consecutive days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party with thirty (30) days' effect, and no Termination Amount is payable by either Party on such termination.

## 11. Change in Law

11.1 Change in Law means the enactment, amendment, repeal or change in interpretation by a court or authority of any Danish or European Union law, regulation, grid code or tariff after the Signing Date, including any change in the rules governing Guarantees of Origin, grid tariffs or the taxation of electricity generation, but excluding any change in corporate income tax of general application and any change published in final form before the Signing Date.

11.2 If a Change in Law increases the cost to a Party of performing this Agreement by more than EUR 250,000 in any Contract Year, the Parties shall negotiate in good faith an adjustment to restore the economic balance of this Agreement, which may include an adjustment to the Contract Price. The Party invoking this section shall document the cost increase.

11.3 If the Parties have not agreed an adjustment within ninety (90) days after the notice invoking section 11.2, the affected Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the other Party with sixty (60) days' effect, and no Termination Amount is payable by either Party on such termination.

## 12. Events of Default

12.1 Each of the following is an Event of Default in respect of a Party (the Defaulting Party, the other Party being the Non-Defaulting Party):

(a) the Party fails to pay an amount due under this Agreement and the failure continues for ten (10) Business Days after written notice of non-payment from the other Party;

(b) the Party fails to deliver, restore or replace credit support under section 9 within ten (10) Business Days after written notice;

(c) the Party is declared bankrupt, enters into reconstruction or liquidation proceedings, or is unable to pay its debts as they fall due;

(d) the Party breaches a material obligation under this Agreement, other than one referred to in (a) to (c), and fails to remedy the breach within thirty (30) days after written notice;

(e) the Party assigns or transfers this Agreement in breach of section 16;

(f) in respect of the Seller, the Commercial Operation Date has not occurred by the Longstop Date, as extended under section 3.5, or the Seller sells electricity generated by the Facility to a third party in breach of section 4.1; and

(g) in respect of the Buyer, the Guarantor becomes subject to an event of the kind described in (c), or the Parent Company Guarantee ceases to be valid and enforceable and is not replaced under section 9.5.

12.2 An Event of Default is not treated as occurring to the extent that the relevant failure is caused by Force Majeure or by a breach by the other Party.

## 13. Termination and Termination Amount

13.1 This Agreement enters into force on the Signing Date and, unless terminated earlier under its terms, ends at the end of the Supply Period.

13.2 While an Event of Default continues, the Non-Defaulting Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice to the Defaulting Party specifying the Event of Default and an early termination date not less than ten (10) and not more than thirty (30) days after the notice. The Non-Defaulting Party may also suspend its own performance, other than payment for electricity delivered, while the Event of Default continues.

13.3 The Termination Amount shall be the present value of the difference between the Contract Price and the Reference Forward Price for the Expected Annual Production over the remainder of the Supply Period, discounted at the reference rate of the Danish central bank plus two (2) percentage points, and determined by the Non-Defaulting Party as at the early termination date, acting in good faith and in a commercially reasonable manner. Where the early termination date falls before the Commercial Operation Date, the remainder of the Supply Period is measured from the Target Commercial Operation Date.

13.4 If the Termination Amount represents a loss for the Non-Defaulting Party, the Defaulting Party shall pay it to the Non-Defaulting Party within twenty (20) Business Days after receiving a statement showing the calculation and the quotations used. If it represents a gain for the Non-Defaulting Party, no Termination Amount is payable by either Party.

13.5 The Termination Amount is in addition to amounts accrued and unpaid at the early termination date, but is the exclusive remedy for the loss of the bargain.

13.6 Termination under sections 2.3, 3.6, 10.4 or 11.3 does not give rise to a Termination Amount. On any termination the credit support is released once all amounts due have been paid.

13.7 Sections 7, 13, 14, 18, 19, 20 and 21 survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement.

## 14. Limitation of Liability

14.1 Neither Party is liable to the other for loss of profit or any indirect or consequential loss, except to the extent included in the liquidated damages, the shortfall payment or the Termination Amount.

14.2 The aggregate liability of each Party under this Agreement in any Contract Year, excluding the Termination Amount, liquidated damages under section 3.4 and payment for electricity delivered, is limited to EUR 2,000,000.

14.3 The limitations in this section 14 do not apply to liability for fraud, wilful misconduct or gross negligence.

## 15. Insurance

15.2 The Seller shall provide the Buyer with certificates of insurance on request and shall notify the Buyer of any cancellation or material reduction of cover.

## 16. Assignment and Lender Protections
